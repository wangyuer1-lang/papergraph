Open papergraph.exe. Your work is saved automatically in Data.

Click a point, ◎ or □ to edit it on the right.
The upper two thirds hold the proposition; the lower third holds notes.
Only proposition text changes the size of a point. Notes can be as long as you need.
Add a short Title above the proposition or arrow settings to label a point or relation.
Press Space on the graph to show titles; press again to hide them.
Titles stay at least 16 screen pixels and grow when zooming in. They never hide automatically and start hidden.
Titles choose nearby spaces to avoid arrows, points and other titles. Pan and zoom keep their chosen positions; graph edits only move them for substantial obstructions.
Titles do not change point size. Click a visible title to select its object.

Left-drag empty canvas to pan. Use the wheel to zoom around the pointer.
Use the arrow keys to select nearby points. Enter edits; Esc returns to the graph.
While typing, arrow keys move the caret normally.
Right-drag to select points and fully enclosed □. Drag the selection to move it.
The bottom bar offers □, ◎ and Delete.
Ctrl / Shift click adds to the selection. Esc clears the selection.
R draws a □. Right-drag its border to move it; hold Shift to include its contents.
Double-click a □ or ◎ to enter its board. Back / Esc returns.
Space toggles only titles; holding it does not repeatedly toggle or enter a ◎.
Double-click empty canvas to add a point. Drag its small handle to create a relation.
Click an edge to choose an arrow symbol and a forward, reverse or two-way direction.
Write notes for that relation in the Notes area below the arrow settings.
Ctrl+G surrounds connected points with a ◎; the internal graph stays visible.
Click the ring border for its notes. Drag the border to move the whole group.
The ring grows with its contents. Right-click its border to ungroup.
Z focuses the selection; press again to return. F fits the whole board.
Ctrl+S saves. Ctrl+Z undoes. Ctrl+Y redoes. The sun / moon switches themes.

Existing .yujian files still open. New files use .papergraph.
